\section{Conclusion}

LFVD samples one reusable family object for post-hoc continuous video queries.
At matched updates it improves Lighting fidelity over a control-aware
Pointwise model, but a high-exposure audit shows that this is not an intrinsic
quality guarantee. The stronger result is functional: LFVD follows measured
query paths more faithfully, and its learned reader outperforms linear
interpolation of the same sampled code. Sparse controls reveal a metric
trade-off, MEAD preserves fidelity rather than superior ordering, and
multi-query inference amortizes after enough requests. Identity matches Mix
overall, locating the contribution in family-valued generation and querying.
