# LFVD encoder reframing and locked-test update

## Method naming

- **LFVD** is the family-valued diffusion framework.
- **LFVD-Identity** is Eq. 4 with the family encoder fixed to
  `lambda=0, P=I`.
- **LFVD-Mix** learns the coordinate embedding and square mixer and remains the
  default method shown as LFVD in the main held-out table.
- **LFVD-Compact** uses a rectangular mixer to shorten the generated code.

The main methodology is now written at the framework level. A generic family
encoder produces one code of length `J'`, one diffusion trajectory samples that
code, and one continuous reader evaluates it at arbitrary controls. Identity,
Mix, and Compact are presented as encoder instances of this shared definition,
not as separate algorithms. The query map, flow-matching objective, inference
path, and cost equations are all expressed in terms of the generic `J'`.

Figure 2(a) now uses the same symbols and stages as the text: the frozen VAE is
`mathcal E`, the central block is the generic family encoder `E_phi` with the
Identity/Mix/Compact instances, and the displayed objective is only
`L_codec`. Separate family-code flow matching remains described in the caption
and Eqs. 7--8; the reconstruction and flow losses are never drawn or optimized
as a sum. The Limitations section explicitly restricts encoder-specific
empirical claims to the three evaluated instances.

## New no-training sparse test audit

All existing five-fold sparse checkpoints were sampled on the locked test
records with seeds 100/200/300 and 50 steps. The audit covers 20 objects and 80
families. At the six controls omitted from training:

| Method | LPIPS | PSNR | SSIM |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pointwise shared-noise | 0.410330 | 18.8123 | 0.723181 |
| LFVD-Mix | 0.341027 | 21.2215 | 0.748012 |
| LFVD-Identity | 0.341689 | 21.1710 | 0.747081 |

LFVD-Mix minus Pointwise has LPIPS -0.069303 (95% CI
[-0.082509,-0.057578]), PSNR +2.40923 dB ([2.12691,2.69008]), SSIM
+0.024831 ([0.019132,0.030457]), and all-control Spearman +0.38618
([0.31569,0.46382]). LFVD-Identity and LFVD-Mix are tied overall. At unseen
controls, Identity minus Mix is +0.000663 LPIPS (CI includes zero), -0.050516 dB
PSNR (95% CI [-0.099801,-0.002076]), and -0.000930 SSIM (CI includes zero).

## Representation audit

All five sparse codes are `[1,48,13,16,16]`, matching
`1 + 3 * (5 - 1) = 13` latent frames. This is 64.9% fewer frames than the
dense 37-frame Lighting code and 2.6 times an ordinary five-frame latent. The
existing 2.37-query sparse/MEAD FLOP crossover is analytical, not wall-clock.
The sparse model attains 0.327125 all-control LPIPS on the locked test, close to
the dense LFVD-Mix result of 0.3216, so the shorter representation's quality and
analytical amortization point are now stated together in the paper.

- `cvpr_sparse_test_jointconcat_5fold_v1_20261103.tar.gz`

Both archives include protocol metadata, per-query and per-family records,
paired object-bootstrap intervals, and SHA-256 manifests.

Legacy public artifact aliases are retained for reproducibility:
`joint_concat_learned_query` denotes **LFVD-Identity**, while `lfvd` denotes
**LFVD-Mix**. Script and archive names containing `joint_concat` refer to the
LFVD-Identity evaluation and do not define a separate method.
